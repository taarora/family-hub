Family Calendar

A Shortcuts Automation reads your Calendar natively (no Apple CORS issues) and pushes events here on a schedule. Set this and the ICS link below becomes a fallback. Full Shortcut build steps in SETUP.md.
Works without any Shortcuts setup, but Apple's calendar servers block most cross-origin/proxy fetches, so this path can be unreliable — the Shortcuts feed above is the sturdier option. iOS Calendar app → your shared calendar → Share Calendar → toggle Public Calendar on → copy the link.

Markets / Open Trades

The Markets tab shows a read-only card for each open trade (same numbers as the trading app — closing/rolling/assigning still happens there, not here). Only reachable while your Mac and the trading server are on and this device is on the same Wi-Fi. See SETUP.md.

Recipes / Mealie

The Recipes tab embeds your Mealie site — full recipe search, meal planner, and shopping lists. Only reachable while your Mac and Mealie are on and this device is on the same Wi-Fi (same limitation as Open Trades above). See SETUP.md.

Sync across devices (Firebase)

Groceries, to-dos and recipes sync live between your iPad/iPhone/laptop through a free Firebase project. Paste the config from your Firebase console (Project settings → your web app → SDK setup and configuration). Full steps in SETUP.md.
